About the App

Cute Daisy Wallpapers is a wallpaper app that provides cute daisy flower wallpapers for Android devices. The app lets users browse wallpapers, open selected images, share wallpapers, and set wallpapers as the home screen, lock screen, or both where supported by the device.

No account or login is required to use the app.

Information We Collect

Cute Daisy Wallpapers does not ask users to provide their name, phone number, email address, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

When you set or share a wallpaper, the app may create a temporary local copy of the selected image on your device. These wallpaper files are not uploaded to a READY COM server by the app.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use this information only to respond to support, privacy, or app-related requests.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Cute Daisy Wallpapers uses third-party advertising services to show ads and measure ad performance.

The app uses Unity LevelPlay / ironSource mediation. Ads may be served through Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, and other mediation partners depending on availability and configuration.

Advertising partners may collect, process, or share information such as advertising identifiers, app set identifiers, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device model, operating system, network information, app interactions, ad views, ad clicks, diagnostics, crash-related data, and information used for fraud prevention and ad delivery.

This information may be used to deliver ads, personalize ads where allowed, measure ad performance, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud, and keep advertising services secure.

For more information, please review the privacy policies of our advertising partners:

  • Unity / LevelPlay / ironSource: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Sharing and Setting Wallpapers

When you share a wallpaper, the receiving app or service handles the shared image according to its own privacy policy.

When you set a wallpaper, Android handles the wallpaper change on your device.

Permissions

The app may use wallpaper-related permission to set selected images as your device wallpaper where supported.

Internet access is used for advertising and related services.

Advertising ID access may be used by advertising SDKs for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and frequency capping.

Children’s Privacy

Cute Daisy Wallpapers is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Data Retention and Deletion

Temporary files and app data stored locally remain on the device until removed by Android, cleared by the user, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

Advertising partners manage the retention of information they collect according to their own privacy policies.

Users may clear app data or uninstall the app from Android settings.

Your Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to reset or delete your advertising ID or change ad personalization settings in Android settings.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information that READY COM may hold from support communications.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.
